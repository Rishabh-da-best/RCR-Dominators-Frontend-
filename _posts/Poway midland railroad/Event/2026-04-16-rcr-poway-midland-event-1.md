  <h1>Steam Every Saturday!</h1>

    <p>Starting in 2026, our beloved <strong>1907 Baldwin Steam Locomotive</strong> will run every single Saturday throughout the season. This marks the first time in over a decade that steam operations will be offered on a weekly basis.</p>
    
    <h2>Schedule Details</h2>
    <p>Trains depart every hour from <strong>10:00 AM to 4:00 PM</strong>. Each ride lasts approximately 25 minutes and covers 1.5 miles of scenic track through the historic Midland corridor.</p>
